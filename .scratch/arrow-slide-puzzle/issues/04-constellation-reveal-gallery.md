# 04: Constellation reveal and gallery

**What to build:** Every level hides an original constellation under a frosted overlay. Clearing a ribbon punches a soft hole along the path it vacated. The last ribbon dissolves the frost, draws the constellation, names it, and adds it to a gallery; uncollected entries appear as silhouettes.
